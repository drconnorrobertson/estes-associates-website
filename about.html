Our Story

About Estes & Associates LLC

Locally owned and operated in central Missouri for over 30 years, built on a foundation of honesty, accuracy, and genuine care for our clients.

Who We Are

Estes & Associates LLC is a full-service accounting firm headquartered in Jefferson City, Missouri's state capital. Founded and led by Kari Estes, our firm has spent more than three decades helping individuals, families, and businesses across central Missouri navigate taxes, bookkeeping, payroll, and IRS problems.

We are not a national chain or franchise. We are a locally owned, independent firm with deep roots in this community. Our clients are our neighbors, and we treat them that way. When you call us, you speak to someone who knows your name and your situation.

Our Mission

"Provide the highest level of honest and accurate financial information to our valued clients through open communication, integrity, and confidentiality, in an environment that stimulates growth and exceeds expectations."

What Sets Us Apart

We are experts in Missouri and federal tax problems. Beyond standard accounting and bookkeeping, our team is skilled in complex IRS resolution matters including offers-in-compromise, innocent spouse relief, wage garnishments, liens, payroll tax problems, and Missouri State licensing issues under HB600.

We do more than run the numbers. We take time to explain what is happening in plain language, educate our clients on their options, and help them make decisions they feel confident in. That philosophy of education-first has built lasting relationships with clients who return year after year.

About Kari Estes

Kari Estes founded and leads Estes & Associates with a commitment to professional excellence and community service. In 2022, she was honored by the Missouri Women's Business Center with the "Women Who Own It" Award, recognizing her leadership and business achievement in Missouri.

Kari is a member of multiple professional organizations and stays current on the latest federal and state tax developments to ensure clients always receive informed, up-to-date advice.

Payment Methods Accepted

We accept Cash, Check, Visa, MasterCard, and Discover. Financing and payment plans are available with prior approval so cost is never a barrier to getting the help you need.
