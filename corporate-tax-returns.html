Corporate Tax Returns

Accurate, compliant corporate tax return preparation for Missouri businesses of all structures, with strategic tax planning built in.

Business Tax Return Preparation in Missouri

Estes & Associates prepares federal and Missouri state tax returns for all business entity types. Whether you operate as a sole proprietor, LLC, S-Corp, C-Corp, or partnership, we ensure your returns are accurate, filed on time, and optimized for your situation.

Entity Types We Serve

  • C-Corporations (Form 1120)
  • S-Corporations (Form 1120-S)
  • Partnerships and LLCs (Form 1065)
  • Sole Proprietorships (Schedule C)
  • Multi-entity business structures
  • Estates and trusts with business interests

Missouri State Tax Compliance

Missouri has specific corporate income tax and franchise tax rules that require careful attention. Our team is experienced with Missouri Department of Revenue requirements and keeps current on any changes to state tax law that affect your business. We also handle multi-state returns for businesses operating across state lines.

Strategic Tax Planning

We do not just prepare what happened. We work with you during the year to plan what should happen. That includes retirement plan contributions, entity structure evaluation, timing of income and expenses, and other strategies that reduce your tax burden legally and proactively. Pair your corporate returns with our full-service accounting for a seamless year-round partnership.

Payroll Tax Compliance

Corporate clients with employees also benefit from our payroll services, which include quarterly 941 filings, year-end W-2 preparation, and state payroll tax deposits. We coordinate payroll and corporate returns for a complete picture.

Estes & Associates has resolved complex payroll tax problems resulting in over $250,000 in penalties being eliminated for Missouri business owners.

IRS Problems for Businesses

If your business has unfiled returns, payroll tax debts, or an active IRS audit, we handle that too. See our tax preparation and management page for a full list of IRS resolution services.
