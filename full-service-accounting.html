Our Services

Full-Service Accounting

Complete bookkeeping, financial reporting, and tax preparation for individuals and businesses throughout central Missouri.

What Full-Service Accounting Includes

Whether you are a sole proprietor, an LLC, or a growing corporation, Estes & Associates provides the full range of accounting services that keep your financial records accurate and your obligations met. We serve clients in Jefferson City, Columbia, St. Robert, Richland, Lebanon, and across Missouri.

Bookkeeping

Organized, up-to-date books are the foundation of every healthy business. Our bookkeeping services maintain your accounts, categorize transactions, reconcile bank and credit card statements, and keep your financial records current month by month. Accurate bookkeeping also makes tax time significantly easier and less stressful.

Financial Statement Preparation

We prepare income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements that give you and your stakeholders a clear picture of where your business stands. Whether you need statements for a bank loan, investor presentation, or internal planning, we produce professional-grade documents that hold up to scrutiny. Learn more about our dedicated financial statement services.

Income Tax Preparation

Our team handles all aspects of income tax preparation for individuals, businesses, partnerships, S-Corps, C-Corps, estates, and trusts. We identify every legitimate deduction and apply current tax law to minimize your liability. See our pages on corporate tax returns and personal tax returns for more detail.

Year-Round Advisory

Unlike firms that only surface at tax season, we are available year-round to answer questions, review major financial decisions, and help you plan ahead. Proactive communication is a core part of how we serve clients.

"Whether you're an individual or a company, we can handle your bookkeeping, financial statement preparation, and income tax filing from start to finish."
