Not-For-Profit Accounting

Specialized accounting, Form 990 preparation, and compliance support for Missouri nonprofits, churches, and charitable organizations.

Nonprofit Accounting That Keeps You Compliant

Nonprofit organizations face unique accounting and tax compliance requirements. Maintaining tax-exempt status under IRC Section 501(c)(3) or other subsections requires careful recordkeeping, proper financial reporting, and timely IRS filings. Estes & Associates provides specialized accounting services to meet those obligations.

Who We Serve

  • Churches and religious organizations
  • Civic and social welfare organizations
  • Trade associations and professional societies
  • Foundations and grant-making organizations
  • Community service and youth organizations

Form 990 Preparation

The Form 990 is the IRS's primary information return for tax-exempt organizations. It must be filed annually and is publicly available, meaning donors, grantors, and the public can review it. An accurate, complete 990 builds credibility and protects your tax-exempt status. We prepare Form 990, 990-EZ, and 990-N depending on your organization's size and activity.

Nonprofit Financial Statements

Nonprofit financial reporting follows different standards than for-profit accounting. We prepare statements of financial position, statements of activities, and statements of cash flows in accordance with GAAP for nonprofits. Many grantors and boards require audited or compiled statements before approving funding.

Losing tax-exempt status can be devastating for a nonprofit. Timely and accurate IRS filings are not optional, and an experienced accounting partner makes compliance manageable.

Payroll for Nonprofits

Nonprofits with employees have the same payroll tax obligations as for-profit entities. Our payroll services cover nonprofit employers with accurate withholding, quarterly filings, and year-end W-2 distribution.

Bookkeeping and Fund Accounting

Many nonprofits use fund accounting to track restricted and unrestricted funds separately. We help establish and maintain bookkeeping systems that correctly segregate funds so your board, donors, and auditors have clarity on how resources are used. This connects with our broader full-service accounting work.
