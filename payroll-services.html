Full-Service Payroll

Reliable payroll processing tailored to your business, so you never miss a payroll obligation or pay a penalty.

Payroll Processing for Missouri Businesses

Payroll is one of the most regulated aspects of running a business. Missed deadlines, incorrect withholdings, and late deposits carry real penalties. Estes & Associates handles payroll processing for businesses of all sizes, from a single employee to dozens of staff members.

What Our Payroll Service Covers

  • Accurate calculation of wages, salaries, and overtime
  • Federal, Missouri, and local income tax withholding
  • Social Security and Medicare (FICA) withholding and employer matching
  • Direct deposit setup and processing
  • Quarterly 941 payroll tax return filing
  • 940 annual FUTA filing
  • Missouri quarterly UI-3/40 employer reports
  • Year-end W-2 and W-3 preparation and filing
  • New hire reporting to the Missouri Division of Employment Security

Customized to Your Business

There is no one-size-fits-all payroll solution. We personalize our services based on your pay frequency, employee mix, benefits deductions, and state obligations. Whether you run weekly payroll for a construction crew or semi-monthly payroll for an office, we handle it consistently.

Payroll tax problems are among the most costly IRS issues a business can face. Getting payroll right from the start is far less expensive than resolving penalties after the fact.

Payroll Tax Problem Resolution

If your business has accumulated payroll tax debt, trust fund penalties, or has received IRS notices related to payroll, Estes & Associates can help. We have a strong track record resolving payroll tax issues for Missouri business owners. See our tax management page for more on IRS resolution services.

Pair Payroll With Full Accounting

Many clients use our full-service accounting alongside payroll to maintain completely integrated financial records. We also coordinate corporate tax returns so that payroll data flows correctly into your annual filing.
