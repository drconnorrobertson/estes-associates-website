Personal Tax Returns

Expert individual tax preparation for Missouri residents, focused on accuracy, maximum legal deductions, and year-round support.

Individual Tax Preparation in Missouri

Filing a tax return accurately matters more than most people realize. A missed deduction costs money. An error can trigger an IRS notice. Estes & Associates prepares individual federal and Missouri state returns with precision, identifying every deduction and credit you are entitled to claim.

Who We Help

  • W-2 employees with standard and itemized deductions
  • Self-employed individuals and freelancers
  • Rental property owners (connect with our property management accounting)
  • Retirees with Social Security, pension, and investment income
  • Individuals with complex investments or stock options
  • Divorced or separated individuals navigating dependent and alimony issues
  • Clients with prior year unfiled returns

Missouri State Tax Filing

Missouri has its own income tax rates, deductions, and credits separate from federal law. We prepare Missouri state returns alongside federal returns, ensuring both are optimized and consistent. We also handle returns for neighboring states including Illinois and Kansas.

Earned Income and Other Credits

Credits like the Earned Income Tax Credit, Child Tax Credit, education credits, and energy efficiency credits can significantly reduce your tax bill or increase your refund. We review your full situation to apply every credit you qualify for.

Many Missourians leave money on the table simply because their prior tax preparer did not ask the right questions. We do.

IRS Problems on Personal Returns

If you have received an IRS audit notice, a wage garnishment, or a tax lien on personal income, we handle resolution as part of our tax preparation and management services. We also assist with innocent spouse claims and amended returns.
