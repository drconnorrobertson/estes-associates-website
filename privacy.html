Privacy Policy

How Estes & Associates LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

Estes & Associates LLC collects information you provide directly to us, such as your name, contact information, and financial information necessary to provide accounting and tax services. We may also collect information through our website's contact forms and client portal.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide accounting, tax preparation, payroll, and related services to you. We also use information to communicate with you about your account, send important notices, and respond to your inquiries. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Confidentiality

As a professional accounting firm, we are bound by professional ethics and applicable law to maintain the confidentiality of client financial information. All client data is treated with strict confidentiality and protected by reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards.

Information Sharing

We do not share your personal or financial information with third parties except as necessary to provide services you have requested, as required by law (such as tax filings with the IRS or state tax authorities), or with your explicit written consent.

Data Security

We maintain security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Our client portal uses encrypted connections to protect document transmission. Despite these measures, no security system is completely impenetrable.

Your Rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request deletion of your information subject to our legal and professional obligations.
