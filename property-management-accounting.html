Property Management Accounting

Dedicated accounting support for Missouri property managers and real estate investors, from rental income tracking to tax filing.

Accounting for Property Managers and Investors

Managing rental properties creates unique accounting needs. Tracking income by property, managing security deposits, reconciling owner distributions, and staying compliant with Missouri landlord-tenant laws all require organized, property-specific bookkeeping. Estes & Associates provides accounting services designed for this specialized context.

What We Provide

  • Rental income tracking by property and unit
  • Expense categorization for maintenance, repairs, insurance, and management fees
  • Security deposit accounting under Missouri law
  • Owner statement preparation for investors and property management companies
  • Monthly and quarterly financial reporting by property
  • 1099 preparation for vendors and contractors
  • Real estate tax return preparation (Schedule E and Form 8825)
  • Depreciation tracking and recapture analysis

Real Estate Tax Returns

Rental income is taxable, and so are gains from property sales. We prepare the complete real estate portions of individual and business tax returns, including Schedule E for individuals, Form 8825 for partnerships and S-Corps, and related depreciation schedules. Proper depreciation tracking can significantly reduce your taxable rental income each year.

Real estate investors who do not track depreciation accurately often either overpay taxes or face a large depreciation recapture bill when they sell. We make sure your records are correct from the start.

Multiple Property Owners

If you own multiple properties, consolidated reporting becomes essential. We organize your accounting so you can see the performance of each property individually and as a portfolio, giving you the information needed to make sound decisions about which properties to keep, improve, or sell.

Connecting With Other Services

Property management clients often benefit from our financial statement preparation, corporate tax returns for properties held in LLCs, and our full-service accounting for an integrated solution across all your financial activity.
