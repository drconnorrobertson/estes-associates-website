Our Services

Tax Preparation & Management

Full-spectrum tax preparation and IRS resolution for individuals, businesses, and organizations across Missouri.

Comprehensive Tax Services for Every Situation

Tax preparation is only part of what we do. Estes & Associates handles the full range of tax and IRS issues that individuals and businesses face, from routine annual filings to complex multi-year IRS problems. With over 30 years of experience, we have seen virtually every tax situation and know how to resolve them.

Tax Return Preparation

We prepare individual, corporate, partnership, estate, trust, and nonprofit tax returns for clients throughout central Missouri. See our dedicated pages for personal tax returns and corporate tax returns for more detail.

IRS Problem Resolution

When the IRS comes knocking, we answer on your behalf. Our IRS resolution services include:

  • Offers in Compromise (settling tax debt for less than owed)
  • Innocent Spouse Relief claims
  • Installment agreement negotiation
  • Penalty abatements and appeals
  • IRS audit reconsideration and representation
  • Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests
  • Unfiled income tax return resolution
  • Amended return preparation
  • Bankruptcy evaluation and support

Collection Action Defense

When the IRS moves from notices to action, the situation becomes urgent. We assist clients facing wage garnishments, bank levies, property seizures, and federal tax liens. Stopping collection action requires prompt, knowledgeable representation and that is exactly what we provide.

Estes & Associates recovered over $40,000 in funds taken by the IRS for one client, and eliminated over $250,000 in payroll tax penalties for another. Results depend on your specific situation, but we fight hard for every client.

Missouri State Tax Problems

Beyond the IRS, we handle Missouri state tax resolutions including state audit defense, Missouri Department of Revenue installment agreements, and Missouri State licensing problems under HB600.

Payroll Tax Problems

Unpaid payroll taxes and trust fund recovery penalties are among the most aggressive collection targets for the IRS. If your business has payroll tax debt, connect with our team immediately. Early action significantly improves outcomes. This service complements our payroll processing and corporate tax services.
